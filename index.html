01 — About

Who We Are

Kurai is a team founded in October 2024 with a clear and ambitious vision: to grow steadily over time, build a strong identity, and earn recognition on a global scale. Our ultimate goal is to compete at the highest level in Rocket League, reaching the most prestigious tournaments and proving our value among the best teams in the scene.

Beyond competitive success, Kurai is committed to building a strong and recognizable brand. We strive to increase our popularity while maintaining authenticity, creating a community where players and supporters feel genuinely connected. Every member of the team is supported both individually and collectively, ensuring they have the resources, motivation, and environment needed to achieve their personal and competitive goals.

We believe that communication is key to growth. That's why we actively encourage questions, feedback, and interaction from our community. By staying open and engaged with our supporters, we aim to create a loyal and passionate fanbase that grows alongside us. Kurai is not just a team—it's a project built on ambition, dedication, and connection.
