VFX / GFX

Make it look legendary.

The VFX & GFX division is the creative backbone of Team Kurai's visual identity. These are the artists, designers, and motion editors who shape how the world sees us — through thumbnails, logos, overlays, banners, animated intros, cinematic edits, and everything in between.

Great esports teams are recognized as much by their visuals as by their gameplay. If you have an eye for design and a passion for creating stunning graphics or effects, you'll play a direct role in building Kurai's brand and making every piece of content look as professional as possible.

We use a range of tools — from Adobe Premiere and After Effects to Photoshop, Illustrator, and DaVinci Resolve. We don't require expertise in all of them, but a solid portfolio of work is essential. Whether you specialize in motion graphics, static design, or cinematic video editing — show us what you can do.

Requirements
A portfolio of your work (Behance, Google Drive, social media, or similar). Experience with at least one industry-standard tool (Premiere, Photoshop, After Effects, etc.).
Commitment
Availability to deliver assets on request, regular collaboration with content creators, and responsiveness on Discord for team requests.
What you'll do
Thumbnail design, animated intros/outros, team overlays, social media graphics, logo creation, and cinematic highlight editing.
What we offer
A real portfolio of professional team assets, creative freedom within brand guidelines, and public credit on all produced content.
